The **Cohere Labs Community Blog** is where members of the [Cohere Labs Open Science Community](https://cohere.com/research/open-science) publish research notes, technical essays, community stories, and practical perspectives on AI. We aim to make work easier to discover, reproduce, and build on—aligned with how [Cohere Labs](https://cohere.com/research) approaches transparent research and open collaboration.

## Who this is for

Readers include community researchers, collaborators, and anyone interested in how community-led work is done in practice. Writers are community members who have gone through the internal editorial process described in our contributor guide.

## Contributing

If you are interested in writing for this blog, start with the process and front matter conventions in [**CONTRIBUTING.md**](https://github.com/Cohere-Labs-Community/blog/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) in the [blog repository](https://github.com/Cohere-Labs-Community/blog). Posts typically use the Distill-style layout for longer research pieces, citations, and figures.

- Learn more about the **Open Science Community** on [cohere.com/research/open-science](https://cohere.com/research/open-science).
